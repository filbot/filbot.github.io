This device sits on your desk or wall, displaying a simple challenge: "WYAO" (Work Your A** Off). When you complete your habit (e.g., a workout, a study session, a task), you press the button. The screen rewards you with your current statistics, reinforcing your progress, before silently reverting to its challenge mode.
